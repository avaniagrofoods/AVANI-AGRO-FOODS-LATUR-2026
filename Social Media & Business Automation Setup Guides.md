Create a comprehensive set of step-by-step setup manuals and record-keeping markdown files for **AVANI AGRO FOODS** to register, optimize, and automate its social media, business messaging, and B2B trade platforms.

Sachin Shinde will be able to follow these documents field-by-field to complete setup, and use them as the primary source of truth (records) for business profiles.

## User Review Required

> [!NOTE]
> The setup paths and fields will match the latest interface configurations of each platform (as of 2026). The files will be organized under a new subfolder in the workspace: `documentation/social_media_setup/`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> To ensure consistency across all setups, a **Master Business Profile** record file will be created first. This file will serve as the "copy-paste sheet" containing exact copy, keywords, contact details, HS codes, and company descriptions matching different character limits (e.g., 80, 150, 500 characters).
