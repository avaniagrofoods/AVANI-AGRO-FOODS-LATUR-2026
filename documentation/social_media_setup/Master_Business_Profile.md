## 📦 3. Detailed Product Profiles

Use these specifications when listing products on catalogs, shops, or B2B directories:

### 🌿 Product A: Premium Moringa Leaf Powder
* **Product Name:** Premium Organic Moringa Oleifera Leaf Powder
* **HS Code:** `0712.90.90` (Dehydrated Vegetables, others - including Moringa)
* **Description:** Prepared from freshly harvested, washed, and shadow-dehydrated leaves of Moringa Oleifera. Rich green color, typical pleasant aroma, free from additives or artificial colors.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * Appearance: Fine green powder (Mesh size 80-100)
  * Moisture Content: < 6%
  * Ash Content: < 7%
  * Cold Water Solubility: High
  * Pesticide Residue: Nil / Complies with EU/US FDA norms
  * Microbial Limits: Yeast & Mold < 100 CFU/g, E. Coli & Salmonella: Absent
* **Packaging Options:** Bulk 20kg/25kg HDPE Bags with food-grade inner liners, 1kg Stand-up Pouches for retail-ready B2B orders.
* **Key Selling Points:** High iron, calcium, and protein content; non-GMO; sourced from pure farms in Maharashtra & Southern India.

### 🧅 Product B: Dehydrated Red Onion Powder
* **Product Name:** Premium Dehydrated Red Onion Powder
* **HS Code:** `0712.20.00` (Onions, dried, whole, cut, sliced, broken or in powder)
* **Description:** Made from clean, sorted, dehydrated red onions ground into a highly soluble fine powder. Distinct sweet-spicy onion aroma, excellent flavor enhancer.
* **Key Specifications:**
  * Appearance: Free-flowing pinkish-red powder (Mesh size 80-100)
  * Moisture Content: < 5% (Strictly controlled to prevent caking)
  * Hot Water Insoluble: < 1.5%
  * Additives: None (100% Pure Red Onion)
  * SO2 Content: < 50 ppm (or as specified by importer)
  * Packaging: Air-tight aluminum foil bags inside cardboard cartons (10kg/20kg/25kg) to protect against humidity.
* **Key Selling Points:** Highly soluble, long shelf life (up to 12-18 months under dry storage), no anti-caking chemical additives.
